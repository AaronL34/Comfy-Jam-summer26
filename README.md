# Comfy Jam Summer 2026

## Tools
- GODOT 4.6.3
- Aseprite

## Ideas
- Bullet hell
- Simple RPG
- Collect resources in the day and expend them during the night (CAMPING DURING SUMMER)
- Player can have different comfy animals during the day time and also help druing night

## Themes
- Summer
- Currupton of forest
- End of game you save currupted fella and he becomes comfy
- 3 nights friday sat sunday as your summer weekend

# Gameplay loop
- 80% of the time is day
- 20% of the time is night
- Each animal can do 1 thing, Active and Passive

### What to do during the day
- Collect Resources
    - Items
        - Starter: Rocks
        - Fishing pole
            - Catches Samlon
        - Torches
            - Creates Fire when you throw it
        - Campfire
            - Campfire Effect: Clear out Smaller Enamys
        - Acorns
        - Bones
        - Samlon
        - Wood
        - Water
            - Allows you to refil water bar
        - Empty Caseings   
- Craft simple items
    - Shop - buy things with bullet caseings
      - Lighter - used up each night
      - Fishing pole
      - Acorns
      - Bones
      - Empty Caseings

- Players main wepons are the Animals you Tame
  - Animals you can find and Tame
      - Squirrel - tame with acorn
          - Passive: Allows you to jump
          - Active: Throw Squirrel deal damage
      - Cat - tame with samlon
          - Passive: Dash
          - Active: Low range quick wide slashes
      - Wolf - tame with bones
          -Passive: Sprint
          - Active: Command wolf to attack someone
      - Samlon - fish for it
          - Passive:  Swim
          - Active: Consume to gain HP
      - Bevar - tame with wood
          - Passive: Builds campfires for you
          - Active: Throw bever
      - Fox - tame with squirrel
          - Passive: Dash
          - Active: large sweeping motion all around you
      - Bees - Ask bear to leave and they will help you
          - Passive: Heals you a small amount when your not taking damage
          - Active: bee storm at a location that lasts for some amount of time. enamys that move threw it take DOT
      - Raven - Empty Caseings
          - Passive: Helps collect casings
          - Active: fast stright attack

### What to do at night
- survive each wave that get progressively harder
- defeat bosses
    - last day boss is always the same but first 2 can swtich depending on day.
    - BOSSES
        - BIG BAD: Racoon
            - Attacks: Summon Versions of the other 2 Currupted Animals. Summons trash Creating Enviornmental Changes
            - Passive: Loot player of 2 items
        - Currupted Moose
            - Attacks: Charges at you periodically. Can scream and hunters come to shoot it, bulles can hit you.
        - Currupted Eagel
            - Attacks: Slashes the ground causing areas that slow you. Quickly Decends to Attack you rapidly
        - Bear
    - Smaller Currupted Creates
        - Currupted squirrels - Run up and bite you, Low HP
        - Currupted Foxes - dash at you. Mid HP
        - Currupted Ravens - Steal an item if they hit you, drop the item on death so the player can pick it up

## NIGHTS
  - Night 1: Survive 120 Seconds with rocks
  - Night 2: Survive 120 Seconds and Uncorrupt the animal you get
  - Night 3: Survive 200 Seconds and Uncorrupt the animal you get
  - Night 4: Survive 220 Seconds Final Boss night with racoon and other 2
